Frequently asked questions

How much must I contribute?

The required contribution is {{ setting('contribution_amount_cents')|int|money }}. You may pay in parts; your dashboard shows what remains.

When does my payment count?

Only after our server confirms it directly with M-PESA or the bank. Until then it shows as Pending. A screenshot or browser message is not enough.

What if I paid twice by mistake?

The system blocks duplicate payment references. If money left your account twice, open a support ticket with both references and the finance team will review it under the refund policy.

Will I earn a profit?

Not necessarily. Projects can lose money. Distributions happen only from verified net results and only under the documented distribution rules.

Who can see my ID document?

Only authorised administrators. It is stored privately and every viewing is recorded in the audit log. It is never shown on public pages.

Are the bank balances live?

No. They are organisation-reported and reconciled against bank and M-PESA statements; the Transparency page shows when they were last reconciled.

Is the organisation licensed?

This website does not claim any licence or regulatory approval. Ask the organisation directly for its registration details.

How do I complain?

Use the Complaints page. You get a reference number to track progress.
