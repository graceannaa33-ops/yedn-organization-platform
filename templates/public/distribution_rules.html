What the late-completion rule means

  • Current and future: members who finish paying after the deadline are treated the same as everyone else once they are fully paid.
  • Future only: they share in projects funded after they finished paying, but not in projects that were already funded.
  • None: they do not share in distributions.

How amounts are worked out

  • Only the verified net result of a project (revenue − expenses) can be distributed, minus anything already distributed from it.
  • Eligibility is checked on the record date chosen for each distribution, using verified payments only.
  • Shares are rounded down to the cent. Leftover cents stay with the organisation and are reported.
  • Every distribution needs approval by a second officer before payment, and every payment is recorded in the ledger.
